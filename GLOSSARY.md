# Glossary

**Logo plate** — The full, framed photo mockup of the company logo (portrait image, logo on a slate
wall). It is the source image and is not shown on the site.

**Emblem** — The round gear-ring mark as its own image asset, with transparency outside the circle.
It is the brand mark shown in the header and the footer.

**Legal page** — Either of the two pages required by law, the Impressum and the
Datenschutzerklärung. Each has its own address on the site and shares the theme of the landing page.

**Legal text** — The wording of a Legal page, as supplied by the company and its legal generators. It
is never edited as part of design work; only the company changes it. The Company details that appear
in it are not part of the wording: they are filled in from the one place the Company details are kept.

**Company details** — The facts that identify the company: its name, postal address, phone number,
email address, website, representative and register entry. Each is kept in exactly one place (phone and email in
`Contact`, the rest in `Company`) and
shown from there wherever it appears, on the landing page and in the Legal text alike.

**Phoenix mark** — The phoenix cut from the Emblem as a circle, without the gear ring and with raised
contrast. It exists only to build the Favicon.

**Favicon** — The browser-tab and home-screen icon set, built from the Phoenix mark.

**Partnerrolle** — A way an outside party takes part in developing and validating the Grabenfräse. There
are two: *Anwender & Pilotpartner* (from Bau & Infrastruktur, validates site requirements and operating
conditions with the company) and *Entwicklungspartner* (from Industrie & Entwicklung, develops and
validates mechanical engineering, components and prototype with the company). It is not a job: the
page looks for partners, never staff, so it avoids "Stelle", "Karriere" and "Bewerbung".

**Grabenfräse** — The machine the company develops, and the one name the page uses for it ("die
Maschine" is fine once it is named). Not *Grabenmaschine*, not *Fräsmaschine*.
