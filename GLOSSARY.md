# Glossary

**Logo plate** — The full, framed photo mockup of the company logo (portrait image, logo on a slate
wall). It is the source image and is not shown on the site.

**Emblem** — The round gear-ring mark as its own image asset, with transparency outside the circle.
It is the brand mark shown in the header and the footer.

**Phoenix mark** — The phoenix cut from the Emblem as a circle, without the gear ring and with raised
contrast. It exists only to build the Favicon.

**Favicon** — The browser-tab and home-screen icon set, built from the Phoenix mark.
