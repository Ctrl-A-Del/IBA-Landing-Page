# 11.1: Human review fixes

**What to build:** The fixes the user asked for after walking the assembled prototype. Marcus Auner's
portrait no longer appears twice, Kontakt moves onto the light surface, the footer loses the logo
photo in favour of the emblem and the company name, and two small layout defects from ticket 11 are
closed.

**Blocked by:** 11 (Integration QA pass, resolved)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Type:** enhancement

**Decisions:** DESIGN_PLAN.md §3, round `R6` (amends B6, C12 and C15). They were settled in a grilling
session with the user on 2026-10-16. E4 was settled separately, from a throwaway UI prototype on the

`7427ccc` (round 2, white Kontakt and footer variants) and `e9ce850` (empty commit recording the

## Problem Statement

The user reviewed the assembled page and found three things that make it look unfinished:

- **The same face twice.** The Team section shows Marcus Auner first in its row. The Kontakt section,
  directly below and on the same navy background, opens with the same portrait in the same left
  column. Two copies of one photo stack about a section apart, which looks like a mistake rather
  than a choice.
- **The footer logo looks pasted in.** The footer shows the full logo plate: a tall photo of a
  metallic logo on a slate-blue wall, which sits on the near-black footer as a grey rectangle. It
  stands alone on the left, far from two small legal links on the right.
- **Kontakt and the footer blur together.** The two dark tones are only about 1.07:1 apart, so a 1 px
  hairline is the only thing separating them. That contradicts the page's own rule that
  backgrounds, not borders, give it structure (C12).

Two smaller defects from ticket 11 sit in the same part of the page. At tablet widths the Team cards
have unequal heights, because one name fits on a line and the others wrap. On phones the label
"Marcus Auner · Geschäftsführung" breaks with the middle dot stranded at the end of the first line.

## Solution

- **Kontakt moves onto the light surface** and **loses the portrait**. Marcus Auner is still named
  as the person you call, directly beside the number: his name in display type with
  "Geschäftsführung" on its own line beneath, in the same pattern as the Team captions. His face
  stays on the page once, in Team, directly above.
- **Kontakt becomes two columns from `md` up.** Eyebrow, heading and intro go on the left. On the
  right goes the contact block: the name and role, the orange call button, then the email link and
  copy button below a divider. On phones it stacks heading first, so the "Kontakt" nav link lands on
  the heading and the number is near the top of the screen.
- **The footer moves to the brand navy** used by the other dark sections, and the hairline goes. The
  white-to-navy step now separates Kontakt from the footer. This restores the page's dark/light
  alternation: navy (nav + hero) → light (Verfahren) → navy (Einsatzbereiche) → light (Warum wir) →
  navy (Team) → **light (Kontakt)** → **navy (footer)**.
- **The footer's brand block** replaces the logo photo. It is the emblem disc (the nav's crop of the
  same logo, larger), "Ingenieurbüro-Auner GmbH" set in Raleway as real text, and a line
  "© {current year} Ingenieurbüro-Auner GmbH". The brand block sits left and the legal links right
  from `sm` up; on phones everything stacks and centres. The footer has no address, no trust line,
  no phone and no email.
- **Team cards share one height** within a row at every width where they sit side by side.

## User Stories

1. As a Bauunternehmer scrolling the page, I want to see each person's face once, so that the page reads as deliberately designed rather than duplicated by accident.
2. As a Bauunternehmer reaching Kontakt, I want to see who I will be talking to, named next to the number, so that the call feels like calling a person, not a switchboard.
3. As a Bauunternehmer, I want Marcus Auner's name and role in Kontakt to match how Team presents him, so that I connect the contact person to the face I just scrolled past.
4. As a Bauunternehmer on a phone, I want the "Kontakt" link to land on the section heading, so that I immediately know where I am.
5. As a Bauunternehmer on a phone, I want the call button near the top of the Kontakt section, so that I can call without scrolling past a photo.
6. As a Bauunternehmer on a phone, I want the name and role label to wrap cleanly, so that it does not look broken at narrow widths.
7. As a Bauunternehmer on a desktop, I want Kontakt laid out in two columns, so that the white section does not look empty on the right.
8. As a Bauunternehmer, I want the orange call button to remain the most prominent action in Kontakt, so that calling is still the obvious next step on a white background.
9. As a Bauunternehmer, I want the email link and the copy button to stay readable and usable on the light background, so that the secondary channel still works.
10. As a keyboard user, I want a visible focus indicator on every Kontakt link and button against white, so that I can see where I am.
11. As a keyboard user, I want the tab order in Kontakt to follow the reading order (heading, then call, then email, then copy), so that the page behaves predictably.
12. As a screen-reader user, I want Marcus Auner named once in Kontakt, without a redundant image, so that I hear the contact person once and clearly.
13. As a screen-reader user, I want the copy-to-clipboard status messages to keep being announced, so that I know whether the address was copied.
14. As a visitor, I want Kontakt and the footer to be clearly different surfaces, so that I can tell where the content ends and the page footer begins.
15. As a visitor, I want the page's backgrounds to alternate dark and light all the way down, so that each section reads as its own block without borders.
16. As a visitor, I want the footer to show the company's mark and name as crisp graphics and text, so that the brand looks professional rather than like a pasted photo.
17. As a visitor, I want the footer emblem to match the one in the navigation, so that the brand reads as one system from top to bottom.
18. As a visitor, I want a copyright line naming the company with the current year, so that the site looks maintained.
19. As a visitor, I want the legal links (Impressum, Datenschutzerklärung) still in the footer and still working, so that I can find the legal information.
20. As a visitor on a phone, I want the footer stacked and centred, so that it is tidy at narrow widths.
21. As a screen-reader user, I want the company name announced once in the footer, not once as image alt text and again as text, so that the footer is not repetitive.
22. As a visitor on a tablet, I want the three Team cards to be the same height, so that the row looks aligned.
23. As the company, I want the footer to repeat no phone or email, so that contact details live in one place and cannot drift apart.
24. As the company, I want the footer to carry no invented claims, so that the page stays honest about a machine still in development (C2).
25. As a reviewer, I want screenshots of Team, Kontakt and Footer at phone, tablet and desktop widths, so that I can judge the fix without running the page myself.
26. As the next agent, I want DESIGN_PLAN.md to record these decisions, so that I do not mistake the new layout for a violation of a frozen decision.

## Implementation Decisions

**Kontakt section**

- The section uses the light tone of the shared section primitive instead of navy.
- The portrait and its framed card are removed from Kontakt entirely. Kontakt no longer uses the portrait-card primitive.
- The layout is two columns from `md` up. The left column holds the eyebrow, H2 and intro (copy unchanged). The right column holds the contact block: name, role, call button, divider, email link, copy button, the hidden copy fallback and the status line. Below `md` the two stack in that order.
- The label is two lines: "Marcus Auner" in display type, "Geschäftsführung" beneath in smaller body type, with no middle dot. This mirrors the Team caption pattern. C15's one-line "Marcus Auner · Geschäftsführung" wording is amended in R6. The two lines may be one element with a line break or two elements, provided the visible and accessible text names him and his role exactly once.
- All copy-to-clipboard behaviour (three-step fallback, live region, timings) is unchanged.
- On-dark colours map to on-light colours as follows. The mapping comes from the `prototype/footer-tone` branch (commit `7427ccc`) and needs no new tokens:

  | Element | On navy (today) | On light (11.1) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Eyebrow | `mist-400` | `ink-500` |
  | H2, name line | `mist-100` | `ink-900` |
  | Divider | `border-mist-500/40` | `border-ink-900/15` |
  | Email link | `text-mist-100`, `decoration-mist-500`, hover `white` / `decoration-mist-200` | `text-ink-900`, `decoration-ink-400`, hover `ink-700` / `decoration-ink-700` |
  | Copy button | `border-mist-500/60 text-mist-200`, hover `border-mist-300 text-white` | `border-ink-400 text-ink-700`, hover `border-ink-700 text-ink-900` |
  | Copy fallback address | `border-mist-500/60 text-mist-100` | `border-ink-400 text-ink-900` |
  | Status line | `mist-300` | `ink-600` |
  | Focus outline | white (on-dark helper) | `ink-900` (new on-light helper) |

  The role line takes the light tone's muted ink, consistent with the Team caption's role colour.
- The orange call button is unchanged. It is still the locked CTA primitive with navy text on orange.

**Focus helper**

- The shared focus module gains an on-light export next to the existing on-dark one: a 2 px `ink-900` outline with offset, written as a full literal class string (Tailwind content scan, DESIGN_PLAN §2.5). Its header comment is updated to describe both, and to drop the footer's old `#14171a` from the on-dark description. The footer still uses the on-dark helper.

**Footer section**

- The background is the brand navy `#0a1f2e`, the same as the navy section tone. The top hairline is removed. The element stays a `<footer>` landmark outside `<main>`, not a content section.
- The full logo plate is removed from the footer. The brand block is:
  - the emblem disc, cropped from the same source image the nav crops, at a larger size than the nav. It is decorative (empty alt) because the company name sits beside it as text;
  - "Ingenieurbüro-Auner GmbH" in Raleway, as text;
  - "© {year} Ingenieurbüro-Auner GmbH", where `{year}` is the current year computed at render time.
- Layout: the brand block on the left, the legal links on the right from `sm` up; stacked and centred below `sm`.
- The legal links are unchanged (same targets, labels and focus treatment).
- The footer contains no phone number, email, address or trust line.
- The emblem crop is the nav's. If the crop geometry is shared rather than duplicated, it must be one source of truth used by both. Moving it is allowed but not required.

**Portrait-card primitive**

- It gains a documented option that lets the card fill the height of its grid cell, so the cards in a row share a height. The default stays as it is (no fill), so nothing else changes unless it opts in. The 3:4 crop and object position stay locked inside the primitive.
- Team opts into the fill option. Kontakt no longer uses the primitive.

**Section primitive**

- Its tone documentation lists Kontakt under light. The `alt` tone stays for now: it has no v2 consumer after this ticket, and it is removed in ticket 12 together with the `brand.alt` colour token (R6, Q7).

**Header comments**

- The Kontakt, Footer, Team (fill option), Section and focus-module header comments are rewritten for the new behaviour, so no comment still describes the portrait, the plate, the hairline or `brand.alt`.

**Docs**

- DESIGN_PLAN.md already carries R6 (B6, C12 and C15 amended; §4 rows 7–8 and §4.1 updated). This ticket does not change decisions further.
- Ticket 11 is closed. Its closing note hands **E4, E5, E6/F7** and **F6** to this ticket, so don't edit ticket 11. Record the outcome of each in this ticket's own Comments.

## Testing Decisions

**What a good test is here:** it observes what a visitor or a screen reader gets, meaning the DOM, roles,
names, text and link targets. It never asserts Tailwind class strings. This follows the precedent in
every existing section test. Colour, layout and contrast cannot be observed in jsdom and belong to the
browser pass. Tests encoding decisions that R6 reverses are rewritten, not left alongside the new ones.

**Seam 1: the whole page as the app mounts it** (prior art: the ticket-11 page integration test). New
guards that span sections:

- Marcus Auner's portrait image appears exactly once on the page (in Team).
- The full logo plate is not rendered as a full image anywhere on the page. Only emblem crops remain.
- The footer contains "Ingenieurbüro-Auner GmbH" and a copyright line with the current year, and still no `tel:` or `mailto:` link.
- The existing guards stay green: anchors, images shipped in `public/`, navy-on-orange, phone and mail formats, the digits audit, banned claims, the legal-page hashes and the favicon paths. The "only digits on the page" audit must allow the copyright year, derived from the same clock, not a hardcoded 2026.

**Seam 2: each section rendered alone** (prior art: the Kontakt, Footer and Team section tests):

- **Kontakt:** replace "names Marcus … in the page's shared framed card" with: no image in the section; name and role each present exactly once; in DOM order the heading comes before the call, and the call before the email link. All clipboard, mailto, CTA and no-form tests stay.
- **Footer:** replace "shows the full framed logo plate" and "adds no copy beyond the legal links and the logo plate (no copyright line)" with: the emblem is decorative (empty alt); the company name is text; the copyright line shows the current year; the legal links resolve to files in `public/`. Keep "does not repeat the contact details".
- **Team:** the people, order, captions and identical-crop tests stay. Add one assertion that every Team card uses the fill option, observed through whatever the primitive exposes (for example a data attribute), not a class string.
- **Year:** fake the system clock (Jest modern fake timers / `setSystemTime`) and assert the rendered year follows it.

**Seam 3: the browser QA pass** (prior art: ticket 11's headless Edge script in
`.scratch/design-v2/evidence/11/`). Re-run it against a production build and keep screenshots of Team,
Kontakt and Footer at 375, 768 and 1280 px under `.scratch/design-v2/evidence/11.1/`. Check:

- Kontakt renders on white and the footer on navy `#0a1f2e`, with no hairline between them.
- The orange call button still reads as the primary action against white (its edge against white is about 2.5:1; the navy text on orange stays 6.72:1).
- Team cards are equal height at 768–1279 px.
- The name and role label wraps cleanly at 375.
- At 375 the "Kontakt" nav link lands with the H2 clear of the nav, and the call is visible without scrolling.
- Focus is visible on every Kontakt and footer stop, and the tab order is unchanged apart from the removed portrait.
- No horizontal overflow at any width.

Finally run `npm test` and a production build (`CI=true`, so lint warnings fail as they do in CI).
